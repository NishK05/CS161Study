# Appendix — Channel Economics, With Sources (Aug 2026)

Raw findings behind `00-AKSHIT-1K-PLAN.md`. Numbers verified against live pages Aug 29, 2026.

## Seeking Alpha contributor program
- Official payout structure ([about.seekingalpha.com/article-payments](https://about.seekingalpha.com/article-payments)):
  fixed **$45–65 per exclusive article on undercovered stocks**, **$85 for first-ever
  coverage**, **$65 for first IPO coverage**, plus variable payments tied to Premium/PRO
  readership and comment engagement. $100 minimum payout, paid monthly. Only exclusives paid.
- New-contributor reality: one 2025 contributor reported **$1,300 from 20 articles** at the
  $65 fixed rate ([SA Analyst Connect, May 2025](https://seekingalpha.com/article/4789529-analyst-connect-may-2025-rewarding-engagement-new-competition-and-lessons-from-a-new-analyst)).
- Implication: 4–8 deep dives/month on undercovered names → **$200–600/month** in months 1–3,
  before engagement bonuses. $1k on SA alone ≈ 12–15 articles/month (possible, grindy).
- Time-to-first-dollar: ~4–6 weeks (editorial approval + payout threshold).

## Paid newsletter (Substack / Beehiiv)
- Free→paid conversion: realistic median **~3%**, honest band 2–5%; only ~1 in 5 publications
  clears 5%; top-decile finance can reach ~20%
  ([Yana G.Y. conversion analysis](https://www.yana-g-y.com/p/substack-free-to-paid-conversion-rate),
  [Digital Applied 2026 stats](https://www.digitalapplied.com/blog/newsletter-statistics-2026-data-points)).
- Pricing norms, solo equity research: Innovestor $14.99/mo · $120/yr; Watchlist Investing
  $249/yr; Net Interest $25/mo · $250/yr. Sweet spot for a new analyst: **$15–20/mo, ~$150/yr**.
- Math: $17/mo × ~87% net of Substack 10% + Stripe ≈ $14.80/sub → **~68 paid subs for $1k
  MRR** → ~2,300 frees at 3%, ~850–1,400 at a strong 5–8%.
- Ceiling proof: Dirtcheap Stocks ~$269k/yr solo
  ([Behind the Balance Sheet](https://behindthebalancesheet.substack.com/p/the-substack-gold-rush-whos-winning)).
- Typical time to first $1k MRR: **6–18 months**. Startup cost $0.

## Freelance equity research
- Rates: **$50–60/hr posted starting rate** ([ZipRecruiter](https://www.ziprecruiter.com/Jobs/Freelance-Equity-Research-Analyst)),
  $30–150/hr band by experience ([Upwork](https://www.upwork.com/hire/equity-researchers/)).
  Commissioned junior deep dives: ~$300–1,500/report.
- Cold-start: 1–2 months with no platform reviews; direct outreach to micro-funds and FinTwit
  with published samples converts better than Upwork bidding.

## YouTube / short-form + brokerage affiliates
- Monetization gate: 1,000 subs + 4,000 watch-hours; most channels need **8–12 months**
  ([EarnifyHub](https://earnifyhub.com/blog/side-hustles/youtube-side-hustle-realistic-income-2026)).
- Finance RPM is elite ($10–25+/1k views, [OutlierKit](https://outlierkit.com/blog/youtube-rpm-finance-niche));
  brokerage affiliates ~$20–70/funded account ([CommissionDex/Webull](https://commissiondex.com/programs/webull/)).
- Verdict: wrong timeline for a 6-month goal; use short-form only as newsletter top-of-funnel.

## Coaching (pitch comps + finance recruiting)
- Experienced-pro benchmarks: $150+/hr minimums quoted on WSO mentor threads
  ([WSO](https://www.wallstreetoasis.com/forum/job-search/interview-coaching-and-comp-structure)).
- Credible undergrad peer rate: **$40–80/hr**. $1k/mo = 13–25 session-hours. Seasonal peak:
  Sept–Nov (competitions + recruiting). Time-to-first-dollar: days.

## Compliance corner
- ***Lowe v. SEC*, 472 U.S. 181 (1985)** ([Justia](https://supreme.justia.com/cases/federal/us/472/181/)):
  bona fide financial publications of general and regular circulation are excluded from the
  Investment Advisers Act. Conditions in practice: impersonal advice only; disinterested
  (disclose positions; undisclosed paid promotion violates Securities Act §17(b)); regular
  publication schedule
  ([publisher-exclusion overview](https://www.interactivebrokers.com/webinars/spotlight-publisher-exclusion.pdf),
  [Jacko Law Group guidance](https://jackolg.com/insights/extra-extra-read-all-about-it-guidance-on-registration-requirement-for-publishers-of-newsletters/)).
- One-client bespoke research with tailored recommendations can constitute advisory activity —
  keep deliverables informational.
