# The Akshit Plan — $1,000/month by Month 6

**Written:** Aug 29, 2026. **Goal:** a durable $1,000/month revenue stream, built on the one
proven asset in this repo: the research *process* (primary-source verification, red-teaming,
corrections discipline), not the stock calls, which decay.

**The one-line answer:** don't trade for it — sell the research. Stack three channels that pay
the same skill on three timelines: **Seeking Alpha** (weeks), **coaching** (days), and a
**paid Substack** (months, but it compounds and eventually dominates).

---

## 1. Why not trading (the door we close first)

$1,000/month = $12,000/yr withdrawn as income. Capital required at defensible returns:

| Sustained return | Capital needed | Reality check |
|---|---|---|
| 8% | $150,000 | index-fund-like, with drawdown years |
| 12% | $100,000 | better than most professionals net of fees |
| 15% | $80,000 | elite-tier consistency |
| 120% | $10,000 | what a student account requires — fantasy as a *sustained* rate |

The evidence is one-sided: <1% of day traders persistently profit (Barber/Lee/Liu/Odean,
Taiwan, complete national dataset); of Brazilians who day-traded 300+ days, **97% lost money**
and only 1.1% out-earned minimum wage (Chague et al.); regulator-mandated broker disclosures
show 51–80% of retail CFD accounts losing per period. Prop-firm challenges (FTMO-style): ~4–5%
pass both phases, ~1–7% of takers ever see a payout, and the business model is challenge fees.

**Verdict: with a student-sized account, trading has negative expected income. The skill is
worth more sold than traded.** Full numbers and sources: `../../15BTC` repo,
`01-trading-reality-check.md` (kept there because that repo *was* the trading idea).

## 5. Metrics + kill criteria (in this repo's own tradition)

Track weekly in `plan/log/`. The plan is falsifiable:

- **SA:** 2 consecutive rejections of polished articles → get editor feedback, fix format
  before writing #3. Fixed-payout per article <$45 by month 3 → shift mix toward coaching.
- **Substack:** <400 frees by week 12 despite 12+ posts → distribution problem, not writing
  problem; stop writing more, start threading/cross-posting harder. Paid conversion <1.5%
  after 60 days of paid → price or promise is wrong.
- **Coaching:** zero bookings in 3 weeks of posted offers → reprice to $35/hr or bundle
  (memo review flat-fee $75) before concluding no demand.
- **Global kill:** <$300/month total by month 4 → collapse to the two paying channels and
  re-plan; do not keep watering three dying plants.

## 6. Constraints and compliance (non-negotiable)

1. **Citadel competition (live until ~Oct 24):** AI use is *initial research only, disclosed*;
   the submission must be original human work. Therefore: no publishing or selling of the
   eight-name universe work until after the competition; monetize the *process* on
   *different names*. The submission itself is license-encumbered to Citadel — never resell it.
2. **Publisher exemption (*Lowe v. SEC*, 472 U.S. 181):** unlicensed publishing of stock
   analysis is legal if it is (i) **impersonal** — never tailored to an individual's account,
   (ii) **disinterested** — disclose any positions, no undisclosed paid promotion (§17(b)),
   (iii) **regular circulation** — publish on schedule, not episodically around trades.
3. **Freelance drift risk:** a commissioned report for one paying client edges toward
   "investment adviser" — deliver research and information, never per-client buy/sell
   recommendations, never touch client money.
4. **Coaching drift risk:** coach careers, process, and pitch craft — not securities picks
   for the client's own account.
5. Standard "not investment advice" disclaimer on everything; it helps but does not replace
   the three conditions above.
