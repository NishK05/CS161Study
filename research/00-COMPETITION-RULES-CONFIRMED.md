## STRATEGIC IMPLICATIONS — these change everything
1. **Idea sourcing is worth ZERO.** Every team pitches from the same 8 names. Obscurity
   earns nothing. ALL differentiation must come from analytical depth, differentiated
   data, and a quantified variant vs. a consensus you have actually measured.
2. **3-12 month horizon.** A DCF terminal-value argument is a horizon mismatch and will be
   marked down. You need a DATED CATALYST PATH.
3. **2 pages including appendix** is brutally short. Every sentence must carry weight.
   The model is separate and is where depth goes.
4. Ypsomed, Bachem, Stevanato, Gerresheimer, CoreWeave, Palantir, Nvidia, insurance/
   longevity, convenience stores, Torrid, Destination XL — **all uninvestable here.**
