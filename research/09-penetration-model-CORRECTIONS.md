## CORRECTION 1 — penetration is ~6.1%, NOT 11-12.4%
| | Figure |
|---|---|
| Gallup (self-report), May-June 2026 | 11% currently taking for weight loss |
| **IQVIA-derived actual dispensing** | **~16M US adults = ~6.1%** |

**Why Gallup is wrong:** its ever-used-to-current ratio is **1.36:1**. With 40-65% annual
discontinuation, that ratio should be **≥2.5:1**. Gallup is measuring *identity* ("I'm someone
who takes these"); IQVIA measures *dispensing*.

Build-up (week ending Jul 17, 2026, from Novo's Q2 deck + Lilly disclosures):
- Wegovy franchise ~575k TRx/wk (of which **pill ~265k**)
- Zepbound ~650-700k/wk (derived from Lilly's "6 of 10" share statement)
- **Foundayo ~18k/wk**
- Total anti-obesity ~5.5M scripts/month; diabetes GLP-1 ~6.4M/month
- Cross-check: ~$7.0B quarterly US branded obesity revenue at ~$425 net/script ✓

**=> Halve any consumer-impact estimate that was built off the Gallup 11-12% number.**

## CORRECTION 2 — US obesity prevalence has NOT been measured as declining
- Latest **NHANES: 40.3%** for Aug 2021 - Aug 2023 — **this window PRE-DATES mass adoption.**
  NCHS states the dip from 42.4% is **not statistically significant.**
- Gallup's 36.4% is self-reported and **the arithmetic does not close**: most initiators start
  at BMI 35-40, where ~12% weight loss still leaves them obese.
- **Best estimate of true GLP-1-attributable reduction: 1.3-1.5pp — roughly 40% of what
  Gallup implies.**
=> The "-3.5pp, first decline in history" framing in file 01 is **NOT defensible**. Do not use it.

## CORRECTION 3 — Foundayo is a LAGGARD, not the inflection I assumed
- **~18k weekly TRx at week 15**, versus **oral Wegovy's ~267k at week 30** and >5M cumulative.
- Lilly's own executives called the launch **"slower than anticipated."**
- 36,000 prescribers (up from 8,000); ~1 in 4 new starts.
- ⚠️ **Its $98M Q2 revenue implies >$800/script against $149-349 self-pay pricing — that is
  CHANNEL INVENTORY BUILD, not end demand.** Sharp catch; do not read $98M as consumption.
- **ATTAIN-1 ITT weight loss is 11.1%**, vs tirzepatide's 20.9%.
  => **The oral wave raises user COUNTS while LOWERING weight loss PER USER.**
  Breadth, not depth. Any thesis assuming the oral wave deepens the average effect is wrong.

## ⭐ CORRECTION 5 — THE PERSISTENCE QUESTION IS RESOLVED, AND IT KILLS A TEMPTING THESIS
Earlier research flagged: *"if the pill improves persistence, every consumer number doubles."*
**It resolves the other way.**
| Cohort | 12-month persistence |
|---|---|
| Commercial insurance | **<60% at 12-24 months**; 64.1% discontinued by 2 years; 85% within year two |
| **Military Health System (ZERO cost-share)** | **81.9% at 1 year** (70.7% by a stricter measure) |
- Kaiser: an **obesity-only** indication carries **2.46x the discontinuation odds** of diabetes.
- **=> The persistence gap is COST, not biology or needle-aversion.**
- Weight regain: **60-90% within a year of stopping**; STEP-1 extension shows ~two-thirds regained.
**Therefore:** an oral pill does NOT by itself fix persistence — cost does. And the cost picture
is deteriorating for the commercial/self-pay majority (employers dropping, Medicaid shrinking,
Bridge expiring Dec 2027). **Do not build a pitch on "the pill doubles adherence."**

## THE OUTPUT MODEL — 2030
| Metric | Value |
|---|---|
| Active users, 2030 base | **38M (14.2% of US adults)** |
| Bear / base / bull | **26M / 38M / 55M** |
| Durably 10%+ lighter | **14.5M (5.4% of adults)** |
| Aggregate weight change | **-967M lbs = -1.95% of aggregate US adult weight** |
| Per-adult average | **-3.6 lbs** |
| **Aggregate calorie change** | **-2.70%** |

**A ~2.7% aggregate calorie decline by 2030 is the honest number.** That is far smaller than the
popular narrative and is the single most important sanity check on any consumer GLP-1 thesis.
(Consistent with the independent Cornell/Numerator-based estimate of ~30bps/yr at index level.)

## The three numbers that most change the answer
1. **12-month discontinuation** — purely multiplicative. 35% vs 65% nearly doubles the 2030 stock.
2. **Whether Medicare coverage becomes permanent** — worth 5-8M users. Bridge expires Dec 31, 2027.
3. **Real-world efficacy of the oral mix** — governs how many cross back under the BMI-30 threshold.

## What the market is getting wrong (per this model)
Volume is roughly right (base-to-bull), but **revenue-per-user is too high and the
December 2027 Bridge cliff is not priced.** The tell: **Novo's US obesity sales grew 4% CER
against 87% US obesity market VOLUME growth** — price is collapsing into volume.
