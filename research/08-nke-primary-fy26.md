## ⭐ THE SETUP NOBODY IS FRAMING CORRECTLY
- FY26 actual diluted EPS = **$2.10**. At $38.59 that is **~18.4x TRAILING**.
- Reported forward P/E is **~22.5x**, which implies consensus FY27 EPS of roughly **$1.72**.
- **=> The street is modelling a FURTHER ~18% EPS DECLINE in FY27, on top of the 44% decline
  from FY24's $3.73 and the 3% decline in FY26.**

That is the variant-perception hook: **consensus has Nike's earnings troughing LOWER and LATER.**
The pitch is a bet on the shape and timing of the trough, not on a multiple.

## Channel and category detail
- **NIKE Direct $17.7B vs $18.8B — DOWN**, and the 10-K attributes it "primarily [to] a decrease
  in traffic." NIKE Brand **wholesale $27.5B vs $25.9B — UP.**
  => This is the deliberate strategy reversal (re-embracing wholesale) showing up in the mix.
  Direct is higher-margin, so the channel shift is a gross-margin headwind that NA overcame anyway.
- Category: **Footwear $29,525M (0%, -2% cc); Apparel $13,449M (+4%, +2% cc); Equipment $2,199M (0%).**
  **Apparel is outgrowing footwear** — mildly supportive of the re-wardrobing/activewear angle.
- Concentration: three largest US customers ≈ **29% of US sales**. 347 US retail stores; 641 total.
- No customer was ≥10% of consolidated revenues.

## ⚠️ The yellow flag to investigate
Inventories were **flat in dollars but the 10-K says this reflects "an increase in UNITS, offset
by product mix."** Units up + dollars flat = **lower average cost per unit**, which can signal
mix-down and future markdown risk. This is exactly the kind of detail a Citadel judge probes.
Get the unit/dollar split from the Q1 FY27 call.

## Gross margin bridge FY26 (from the 10-K, +20bps total)
- Lower warehousing and logistics costs: **+~20bps** (channel mix)
- Favorable net FX including hedges: **+~20bps**
- Lower other costs: **+~20bps**
(These three sum to more than +20bps, so there are offsetting negatives the filing lists
elsewhere — pull the full bridge before modelling. Tariffs are the obvious offset.)
