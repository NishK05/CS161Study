**Of the four consumer-exposed names in the universe, only Starbucks names weight-loss drugs
as a risk factor at all.** Nike and Norwegian have never mentioned it in any filing.

## The exact language, and how it CHANGED

**FY2023 10-K:** no mention (confirmed by absence in EDGAR full-text, which covers 2001+).

**FY2024 10-K** — filed Nov 20, 2024, Item 1A Risk Factors — verbatim:
> "Changes in diet (whether due to changes in consumer behavior and eating habits, **use of
> weight-loss drugs**, or other factors) could also influence the demand for our offerings and
> **materially harm our business and results of operations**."

**FY2025 10-K** — filed Nov 14, 2025, Item 1A Risk Factors — verbatim:
> "Additionally, health concerns related to ingredients such as caffeine, dairy, sugar, or
> allergens—whether accurate or not—along with increased litigation, regulation and regulatory
> scrutiny, or taxes on certain food components, ingredients, or additives, could reduce demand
> and harm our results. **Shifts in consumer behavior, including dietary changes or use of
> weight-loss drugs, may also impact sales.**"

## The trajectory — and the honest reading
| Year | Status | Severity language |
|---|---|---|
| FY2023 | absent | — |
| FY2024 | **introduced** | "could... **materially harm** our business and results of operations" |
| FY2025 | retained but **SOFTENED** | "**may also impact sales**" |

Starbucks introduced weight-loss drugs as a risk in FY2024 and then **downgraded the severity
of the language** in FY2025.

⚠️ **BE HONEST ABOUT WHAT THIS IS WORTH.** Two readings, and I cannot distinguish them from the
filing alone:
(a) Management concluded the effect is less material than initially feared, OR
(b) Routine risk-factor compression — the FY2025 risk factors are more condensed generally.
Risk-factor drafting is a legal exercise and is a **weak signal**. This is ONE small
corroborating datapoint, not a thesis. Presented as a thesis it gets torn apart; presented
honestly as "the only company in the universe that discloses this, and it de-escalated the
language" it reads as genuine primary-source work.

## Why it is still useful for a Citadel memo
The rules demand *"compelling tangible evidence... technical rather than purely subjective"*
with *"a predictive component."* Reading three consecutive 10-Ks and tracking how a specific
risk disclosure was introduced and then softened is exactly the kind of primary-source work
that separates a real memo from a summary of sell-side notes — **provided it is framed with
the caveat above.**

## Catalyst to watch
The **FY2026 10-K lands ~mid-November 2026**. If the weight-loss-drug language is RE-ESCALATED
or quantified, that is a genuine signal. Timing note: that is AFTER the Oct 2 first-round
deadline and after the Oct 23 finals, so it cannot be used in the submission — but
**Q4 FY26 earnings (~late Oct / early Nov 2026)** partially falls in the finalist window.
