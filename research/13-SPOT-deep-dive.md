## The variant perception that DOES survive
**VP1 — gross margin has PLATEAUED.**
Q4'25 33.1% → Q1'26 33.0% → Q2'26 33.4% → **Q3'26E 32.9%.** Four quarters, no net expansion.
The Q2 "record" was flattered by a €6M Canadian DST accrual reversal (~13bps) plus cost timing
pushed into Q3. Structural cause, from the 6-K: royalties are *"the greater of a percentage of
relevant revenue and a per user amount"* — **a percentage of revenue cannot operating-leverage
with scale.** Management's 2030 target of 35-40% implies **~+410bps over 4.5 years vs +840bps
delivered in the prior four.** The guided rate of expansion is HALF what the stock re-rated on.

**VP2 — ARPU growth is 100% BORROWED, and the lap dates are known.**
Q2'26 ARPU bridge: price **+€0.49 (+10.7pp)**, mix **−€0.14 (−3.1pp)**, FX −€0.02 → **+7.0%**.
**Strip price and ARPU runs at −3%.**
| Price hike | Laps in |
|---|---|
| Sept 2025 international (€10.99→€11.99, ~2/3 of base) | **Q4 2026** |
| Feb 2026 US ($11.99→$12.99) | **Q1 2027** |
Price contribution goes **+10.7pp → ~+3pp → ~zero.**
No-new-hike FY27 revenue build: **+7.5% vs consensus +13.8%.**
**The tell:** the Sept 2025 hike was announced *with* Q2'25 earnings on Aug 4, 2025 — and the
**Aug 4, 2026 print passed with NO announcement.**

**VP3 — AI cannot rescue the margin.** Because royalties are a % of revenue, AI-generated music
changes *who* gets paid from the pool, not the pool's SIZE — **gross-margin neutral.** Vertical
integration is explicitly repudiated (CPO Söderström: *"Our products are about real artists, not
fake artists"*), and the remix product ADDS royalty cost via consent/credit/compensation.
Deezer data: AI is **>50% of daily uploads (~90k/day, Jun 2026)** but only **1-3% of streams,
85% of those demonetized as fraudulent.** AI is genuinely working on engagement (DJ ~25% of
actives, SongDNA 100M+) and cost (flat headcount 3 years, revenue/employee ~doubled) —
**neither touches the label line.**
