## CHECK 3: Does ANY insurer link GLP-1 to mortality in SEC filings?
Full-text search for "GLP-1" + "mortality", Jun 2025 – Aug 2026: **375 documents,
ZERO insurance / reinsurance / annuity companies.** All matches are pharma/biotech
discussing clinical endpoints (Madrigal, Fractyl, Galectin, Biohaven, Veru, etc.).

Contrast with what the reinsurers publish in their OWN research:
- **Swiss Re:** GLP-1s may cut cumulative US all-cause mortality **up to 6.4% by 2045**
- **RGA:** incretins could reduce mortality **up to 8.8%**; **0.2–0.5% annual mortality
  improvement realized over ~20 years**
- Munich Re, Society of Actuaries, American Academy of Actuaries all publishing on it

**=> A genuine disclosure gap.** Reinsurers publish it as research; nobody books it as a
reserve assumption or discloses it as a risk factor. Longevity-exposed books (annuities,
pension risk transfer) are on the wrong side. **Caveat: catalyst timing is the problem —
this is a 20-year assumption drift, not a 12-month repricing.** Possible catalyst hook:
US life insurers run annual actuarial assumption reviews in Q3.

## CHECK 4: West Pharmaceutical (WST) Q2 2026 10-Q — read directly
Filed July 23, 2026 (accession 0000105770-26-000099), doc `wst-20260630.htm`.

| Item | Finding |
|---|---|
| Mentions of "GLP-1" in the 10-Q body | **ZERO** |
| Mentions of oral drug delivery / shift away from injectables | **ZERO** |
| H1 2026 net sales | **$1,717.2M vs $1,464.5M = +17.2%** |
| Q2 2026 net sales | $872.3M vs $766.5M = +13.8% |
| High-Value Products as % of sales | 48% (vs 46% LY) |
| Customer mix | Biologics 42%, Pharma 24%, West Vantage 18%, Generics 16% |
| H1 capex | $85.9M |
| FY26 guidance in the 10-Q | none given |

**Read-through:** WST is compounding ~17% with HVP mix rising, and discloses nothing about
GLP-1 concentration or oral substitution risk. That is the setup for an undisclosed cliff
IF oral takes share. **But it is not yet evidence of one** — and the honest counter is that
the most potent next-gen agents (retatrutide, CagriSema) are INJECTABLES, so oral may expand
the market rather than cannibalize the device layer.

⚠️ Caveat on method: EDGAR full-text search flagged this filing as matching "GLP-1", but
reading the document found no such mention — the index likely matched an exhibit or the
match was imprecise. Do not cite the full-text hit count as evidence without opening the doc.
