## 1. The fair-use binary ALREADY COLLAPSED — toward "training is fair use"
| Case | Dated event | Holding | For Adobe |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Bartz v. Anthropic** (N.D. Cal.) | Ruling Jun 23, 2025; **final approval Jul 20, 2026** | Training on **lawfully acquired** books = **FAIR USE**. Liability attached only to *pirated acquisition*. **$1.5B settlement** | **NEGATIVE** |
| **Kadrey v. Meta** (N.D. Cal., Chhabria) | Ruling Jun 25, 2025; **interlocutory appeal DENIED Jul 2026** | Training = fair use on this record. **No 9th Cir. ruling until 2027-28** | **NEGATIVE** + kills the near-term catalyst |
| **Getty v. Stability (UK)** | Judgment **Nov 4, 2025** [2025] EWHC 2863 (Ch) | **Getty LOST.** Model weights are **not a "copy."** Getty had dropped its training claim | **NEGATIVE** |
| **Thomson Reuters v. Ross** (3d Cir.) | Argued **Jun 11, 2026**; decision pending | The one live appellate catalyst — but Ross is **non-generative** search AI on Westlaw headnotes; easily cabined | Weak |
| **Andersen v. Stability** | **Trial set Apr 5, 2027** | Only image-gen trial on the calendar | 2027 |

**The critical inference:** the surviving theory is not *"training on copyrighted work infringes."*
It is **"torrenting pirated files infringes"** — a **procurement-hygiene problem with a price.**
Anthropic paid **$1.5B ≈ 1% of its valuation** and moved on.
**A one-time payable toll is not a moat. It is a licensing cost curve any well-capitalised rival
can climb.** SCOTUS is a 2028-29 event at the earliest.

## 4. The counter-case is stronger than the bull case
**(a) Rightsholders DEFECTED TO LICENSING — decisive.**
- **Disney invested $1B in OpenAI** and licensed **200+ characters** to Sora (**Dec 11, 2025**) —
  *while suing Midjourney.*
- **Getty signed a multi-year deal with OpenAI (Jun 21, 2026)** — the same Getty that just lost in London.
- **UMG settled with Udio (Oct 2025); WMG settled both Udio and Suno (Nov 2025).**
=> **Every deal manufactures the "safe data" Adobe's moat depended on being SCARCE. That corpus is
now purchasable, and OpenAI/Google have more capital to buy it than Adobe.**

**(b) The third-party model contradiction — Adobe concedes it in its own 10-K.**
Firefly now fronts **25-30+ partner models**. Indemnity is **narrow** — native Firefly outputs and
*generally available* Google/OpenAI media models only; **excludes beta/preview and customer-API-key
access.** So the flagship app's default experience is **largely UN-indemnified.** FY2025 10-K:
> *"as we offer more third-party AI models in our solutions, we face risks inherent in how
> third-party AI models used in our solutions have been developed and deployed, **including
> situations in which the third party may lack a proper license or consent for the training data
> used for their model**…"* and warns of being *"[required] to change our AI development practices,
> monetization strategies **and/or indemnity protections**."*
**Adobe voluntarily imported the exact risk its moat was built to exclude — because customers
wanted model quality more than indemnity. That is a revealed-preference answer.**

**(c) ⚠️ ADOBE IS NOW A DEFENDANT — and this touches my buyback pillar**
- **Lyon v. Adobe** (filed Dec 17, 2025, N.D. Cal.): alleges Adobe's **SlimLM** models were trained
  on SlimPajama-627B → RedPajama → **Books3** (~191k pirated books). *(SlimLM is a document SLM,
  not a Firefly image model — a good defense on the merits, useless against the marketing damage.)*
- **Hirschberger, Peterson & Small v. Narayen et al. — VERIFIED: Case No. 5:26-cv-05882,
  N.D. Cal. San Jose, filed 06/16/2026.** Stockholder derivative complaint naming Narayen,
  Wadhwani, Chakravarthy, Durn, Costin, Calderoni and the full board. Pleads the "commercially
  safe" statements as false and **cites the proxy deletion as evidence of SCIENTER.**
  **It also alleges Adobe repurchased $26.89B of stock from Mar 2024–Feb 2026 at inflated prices.**
  ⚠️ **That is a direct attack on the buyback pillar of my long thesis. It must be disclosed if we
  pitch ADBE long.** (Derivative suits rarely move stocks; a 10b-5 class action would matter more —
  the agent could NOT verify one exists.)
