## The US onshore wind order cliff: OBBBA + IRS Notice 2025-42
**EVENT.** Two interacting deadlines:
(a) §45Y/48E tech-neutral credits require wind/solar to **begin construction by July 4, 2026**
    (now PASSED) *or* be **placed in service by Dec 31, 2027**;
(b) **IRS Notice 2025-42 ELIMINATED the bright-line 5%-of-cost safe harbor** for all wind and
    utility-scale solar (>1.5MW), for projects starting on/after **Sept 2, 2025**, replacing it
    with a "physical work of a significant nature" test — shifting the standard from work having
    "begun" to work having been **"performed."**

## ⭐ THE NON-CONSENSUS MECHANISM
**Historically EVERY PTC/ITC deadline produced a PULL-FORWARD in turbine orders, because developers
safe-harboured by BUYING EQUIPMENT (the 5% test). Notice 2025-42 KILLED THAT CHANNEL.**
Developers must now show physical work; *"stockpiling turbines without identified projects becomes
significantly riskier."*
**⇒ The usual pre-deadline order surge does NOT happen — and the post-deadline cliff has NO CUSHION.
The Street is modelling GEV Wind off a pattern that no longer exists.**
