### The mechanism — three simultaneous discounts, not one
Under **37 CFR 385.2**, Service Provider Revenue for a Bundle = bundle price × [standalone price of
music ÷ sum of all standalone components]. Spotify engineered every input:
Premium **$12.99** · **Basic $10.99** (music-only tier, exists to set a "standalone music price")
· **Audiobooks Access ~$9.99**
→ SPR = $12.99 × (10.99/20.98) = **$6.80 — a 48% haircut to the royalty base.**
Then **§385.21** gives bundles two more breaks: TCC prong **24.5%** (vs 26.2%) and per-subscriber
minimum **$0.33** (vs **$0.60** Standalone Portable).

### ⚠️ THE ALL-IN POINT — do not get this wrong
Under §385.21 the Subpart C royalty is an **"All-In Royalty Pool" covering BOTH mechanical AND
public performance.** All-In Pool = greater of (% of SPR) or (% of TCC), floored by the per-sub
minimum; then **subtract "Applicable Consideration"** (ASCAP/BMI/SESAC/GMR payments) to get the
Payable Royalty Pool remitted to the MLC.
**⇒ The CRB's 15.35% headline sets Spotify's ENTIRE US publishing cost for interactive streaming.
PRO performance payments are a DEDUCTION FROM that pool, not an addition to it.**
This is the single most common modelling error on this name — the base is bigger than a naive
"mechanicals only" read.

### The sizing
**Closing the bundle loophole = +4.0 percentage points on Spotify's effective all-in US publishing
rate** (~11.3% → ~15.3% of US Premium revenue). €115m ÷ US Premium H1 2026 ≈ €2,894m = **3.97%**;
robust at **3.86%–4.03%** across a 40–55% assumption for the US share of global ad revenue.
**Why only 4pp when the revenue haircut is 48%?** The **"greater of" structure catches** — the
24.5% TCC prong and $0.33 floor partially backstop the payment. **This cuts BOTH ways and matters
for credibility: it caps the loophole's value at ~26% of the publishing bill, and equally means a
headline % increase will NOT flow through one-for-one.**

## ⚠️ CORRECTION: THE COURTS WILL NOT CLOSE THIS — ONLY THE CRB CAN
**Jan 29, 2025 — the MLC's suit was DISMISSED WITH PREJUDICE.** Judge Analisa Torres held §115 and
its regulations are *"unambiguous"*, audiobooks have *"more than token value"*, and
**Premium IS a Bundle. Spotify won the classification fight outright.**
