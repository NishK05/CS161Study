# ⚠️ SELF-CHECK: I OVERSTATED THE PJM "525 MW" DATAPOINT
Verified by me directly, Aug 27 2026, before the red-team agent reported.

## What I claimed
> "PJM's 2028/29 auction cleared AT THE CAP and drew only 525 MW of new generation.
> Three consecutive auctions at the cap produced half a gigawatt. **The bull syllogism
> (datacenter load → high capacity prices → new gas plants → GEV turbines) has an empirically
> broken third link.**"

## The objection that partly defeats it
**PJM is still "working toward returning to its three-year-forward planning cycle."**
The **2028/29 BRA was held July 14, 2026** — for a delivery year beginning **June 1, 2028**.
That is **~22 months forward, not the normal 36.**
**A new combined-cycle gas plant takes 4–5 years to permit, interconnect and build.
It is MECHANICALLY IMPOSSIBLE for new greenfield gas to clear an auction held 22 months
before delivery.** So the 525 MW figure does NOT prove that high capacity prices fail to
call forth new gas. It largely proves that PJM's auction calendar was compressed.

**I presented this as stronger evidence than it is. Correcting it.**

## What SURVIVES, and how to frame it properly
1. **Uprates had time to respond and barely did.** The 525 MW is *"new generation AND uprates."*
   Uprates to existing plants have short lead times and could have cleared. The response was
   still de minimis.
2. **The +5,639 MW of "natural gas" in the auction came from higher accreditation factors and
   unit conversions — NOT new plants.** That part stands and is the cleaner statistic.
3. **⭐ THE REAL POINT IS THE CAPPED PRICE SIGNAL, NOT THE CLEARED QUANTITY.**
   PJM has cleared **at the administrative cap for three consecutive auctions**, and FERC
   accepted a **$325 cap / ~$175 floor for BOTH 2028/29 and 2029/30** — a **nominally DECLINING**
   cap (2027/28 cap was $333.44).
   **A price signal that is administratively capped cannot call forth new entry regardless of
   lead time.** The unconstrained clearing price would have been **$554.72 (~$30B vs the $16.4B
   actually paid)** — i.e. the market wanted to pay ~70% more and was not allowed to.
   **A merchant developer underwriting a 4–5 year, $1,500+/kW CCGT against a capped and falling
   capacity revenue with a hard floor cannot make the numbers work.** That argument is immune to
   the lead-time objection.

## The genuinely decisive tests, and both are DATED
| Auction | Date | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| **2029/30 BRA** | **December 2026** — INSIDE the pitch window | **Last COLLARED auction.** If new entry is again ~zero at the cap, the capped-signal argument is confirmed in public |
| **2030/31 BRA** | ~mid-2027 (verify) | **FIRST UNCOLLARED auction — the real test.** Also closer to a normal 3-year-forward window, so lead time stops being an excuse |

## Lesson for the memo
**Do not cite "525 MW" as proof that capacity prices fail to produce new gas.** Cite instead:
(a) the **administratively capped and declining** price signal against a $554.72 unconstrained
clearing price, and (b) that **uprates and accreditation changes — not new plants — supplied the
increment.** Then point at **December 2026** as the falsification date.
**Anyone who knows PJM will raise the lead-time objection in Q&A. Raise it first.**
