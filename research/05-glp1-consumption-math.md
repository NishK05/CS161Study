# The GLP-1 Consumption Math — the quantitative spine for SBUX / NCLH (2026-08-27)

## ⭐ THE PROPER NUMBERS (use these; reject the lab numbers)
- **The lab number is a TRAP.** Tirzepatide cut an ad-libitum lunch **-59% (wk3) / -72% (wk6)** —
  but that is ONE test meal with no free-living data collected. Do not cite it.
- **Steady-state calorie reduction: -22%.**
- **Cornell / Numerator, published in Journal of Marketing Research, ~150,000 households:**
  | Metric | Effect |
  |---|---|
  | Household grocery spend | **-5.3%** |
  | Higher-income households | **-8.2%** |
  | Savory snacks | **-10.1%** |
  | **Limited-service restaurants** | **-8%** |
- Backing out household dilution / substitution / waste => **~-15% food volume per TREATED adult.**

## The aggregate is small; the CONCENTRATION is 4x larger
- Penetration: **11% Gallup / 12% KFF** (Aug 2026)
- Today: 11.5% x 15% = **1.7% already in the base**
- 2030 at 19% penetration: **2.9%**
- **Incremental = 1.2% spread over ~4 years = ~30bps/yr at the index level**
- Cross-checks: Circana $32bn (1.2% of the $2.6tn pool); KPMG $48bn (1.8%). Consistent.
- **BUT for a single exposed category — savory snacks: 19% x 25% = 4.8% cumulative = ~120bps/yr.**
=> The sell-side "it's small" claim is **right at the index level and wrong by 4x for a
   concentrated single-category operator.** This is the key analytical move.

## ⭐⭐ THE SWING FACTOR — and it is the best pitch angle in the whole GLP-1 complex
> **The entire "smaller than feared" case rests on 50-65% twelve-month DISCONTINUATION.
> If the pill materially improves persistence, every number in the model roughly DOUBLES.**

This is the crux. Orforglipron/Foundayo is an ORAL pill with no needle, no refrigeration, no
titration injection ritual, and $50/mo Medicare access from July 1, 2026. Every known driver of
GLP-1 discontinuation (injection burden, cost, cold chain, pharmacy friction) is reduced by the
oral formulation. **If persistence improves from ~40% to ~60% at 12 months, the effective
treated-and-adherent population roughly doubles WITHOUT any change in gross script counts.**
Nobody appears to be modelling this. It is the single most defensible variant perception found.

## ⚠️ NEGATIVE FINDINGS — these KILL several tempting theses
1. **The alcohol thesis is DEAD.** Gallup (Aug 2026): **54% of US adults drink — FLAT**, and
   **drinks per week ROSE 2.8 -> 3.2.** BUD is **+24.4%** over 52 weeks. The "GLP-1s kill
   alcohol" story is not showing up in aggregate consumption.
   => **Do NOT build the NCLH short on beverage-package attrition.** (The clinical RCTs are real
   but small; population-level consumption has not moved.)
2. **Large portions are NOT the exposure.** Cheesecake Factory **+87.2%** over 52 weeks;
   Texas Roadhouse traffic **+3%**. Chipotle's drop was **salmonella**; Wingstop's was
   low-income macro. The "buffet/big-portion concepts die" thesis is empirically false.
3. **Packaged-food volumes actually STABILIZED:** MDLZ NA vol/mix +1.2%; PEP Frito-Lay NA flat
   (after cutting prices 15% — an elasticity problem, not an appetite problem); GIS Q4 base
   volume +1%; HSY revenue +6.6%. KHC is the laggard at -2.7% NA — but a **610bp spread between
   three companies selling in the same aisles is EXECUTION, not pharmacology.**
   Walmart CEO Furner (Aug 20, 2026): *"grocery... mid single digits with strong unit volume growth."*
