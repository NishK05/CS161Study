## The variant perception that DOES work — four quantified legs
**1. Half the comp is not demand.**
CFO Cathy Smith, Q3 FY26 call, to Zach Fadem, verbatim:
> *"With a 7.9% comp that we drove, **about half of it or a little bit less than half of it was
> due to closures, sales transfer, delivery growth**."*
**Organic comp ≈ 4%, not 7.9%.** NA store count is **−1.9% YoY (18,371 vs 18,734)** after the
~$1bn Sept-2025 restructuring (90% NA), so closed-store volume transfers into the comp numerator.
System transactions are growing low-single-digit and **total revenue actually FELL 1.4%.**

**2. Clean NA margin expansion ≈ ZERO on an 8.1% comp.** The 10-Q bridge:
| Item | bp |
|---|---|
| Sales leverage | +340 |
| Lapping one-time FY25 leadership conference | +120 |
| Tariff refunds | +110 |
| Restructuring | −240 |
| **Green Apron labor** | **−190** |
| **Product mix** | **−100** |
| **Net** | **+30** |
Smith says NA margin ex-tariff improved *"more than 100 basis points"* — which still contains the
120bp conference lap. **290 of the 340bp of leverage was consumed structurally.**
At management's own 3% long-term comp algorithm, leverage is only **~130bp — less than the mix
drag alone.** That is the whole bear case in one line.

**3. ~$0.23 of the $0.85 quarterly EPS is non-recurring.**
Smith volunteered: *"the better proxy for a more normalized Q3 COGS rate is the year-to-date
metric of **32.3%**"* vs **30.3% reported** — 200bp ≈ $186M ≈ **~$0.13 EPS**.
Plus ~$0.10 from a **960bp non-GAAP tax swing (21.8% vs 31.4%)** that reverts to "mid-20s" in Q4.
**Normalized ≈ $0.62, +24% — not the reported +70%.**

**4. Consensus has pulled FY28 into FY27.**
Investor Day (Jan 29, 2026), Smith verbatim:
> *"fiscal 2028 operating margins of 13.5%–15% and earnings per share of $3.35–$4… When China
> moves to a JV structure… approximately $0.15 less."*
China DID move. So management's China-adjusted FY28 EPS = **$3.20–$3.85**.
**Consensus FY27 of $3.12 implies a ~13.4% FY27 operating margin — the BOTTOM of the FY28 range,
a full year early.**
At $108.49 that is **34.8x FY27** and **30.7x management's own FY28 midpoint.**
**Highest forward P/E (36.8x) and EV/EBITDA (25.3x) of any healthy peer while earning the LOWEST
operating margin (10.4% vs CMG 15.3%, MCD 45.7%).** Own history: 23.3x at FY23 year-end.
