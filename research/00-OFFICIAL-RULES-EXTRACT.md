Retrieved 2026-08-27 (7 pages).

## ⚠️⚠️ RULE 10 — THE GENERATIVE-AI RESTRICTION. READ THIS FIRST.
> "While Teams may use artificial intelligence tools for **initial research** in the preparation
> of Submission (**provided such use is clearly disclosed to Sponsor**), Submissions **must not
> be generated in whole or in part by any generative artificial intelligence tool**, including
> without limitation tools and models such as ChatGPT, OpenAI models, GitHub Copilot, and
> Vertex AI. Basic word processing features such as grammar check are permitted, but generative
> AI features such as Copilot in Word may not be used to develop a Submission."

**What this means for how we work:**
- ✅ ALLOWED: using AI for **initial research** — gathering data, finding filings, locating
  transcripts, surfacing evidence, sanity-checking arithmetic — **provided it is disclosed.**
- ❌ NOT ALLOWED: having AI **generate the memo or the model**, in whole or in part.
- **=> THE TEAM MUST WRITE THE MEMO AND BUILD THE EXCEL MODEL THEMSELVES.**
- **=> The team MUST clearly disclose the AI-assisted research to Citadel.**
This repo is therefore a **research dossier + evidence pack**, not a draft submission.

## What the First Round Submission must contain
1. One of: **ABNB, ADBE, CPRT, GEV, NCLH, NKE, SBUX, SPOT** — long or short, **3-12 month
   horizon**. *"Submissions analyzing any other security will be disqualified."*
2. **Investment memorandum, PDF, max 2 pages TOTAL including any appendix.**
3. **An Excel valuation model** (explicitly required, separate from the memo).
4. Per team member: first/last name, school email, school year, **and a current resume**
   — *"a Team may be disqualified if resumes are not submitted for all Team Members."*

## The seven content guidelines (verbatim, abbreviated)
1. Clear summary of the investment thesis + recommendation + key drivers, opportunities, risks.
   **"Present the return profile of the investment."**
2. **"Find and present compelling tangible evidence... This should be technical rather than
   purely subjective. Support for the thesis should involve a PREDICTIVE COMPONENT in addition
   to historical analysis."**  <-- the single most important line in the rules
3. Industry overview and competitive analysis, to the extent relevant.
4. Key competitive strengths/weaknesses — business quality, quality of the management team.
5. Key opportunities and risks; factors that could drive significant upside or downside.
6. **"Present and justify model assumptions; present and discuss the Team's valuation process."**
7. **"Excel model should be clearly formatted and easily understandable."**

## ⭐ THE ACTUAL JUDGING CRITERIA (verbatim)
> "...evaluated by judges from the Harvard Financial Analysts Club and/or Citadel based upon
> criteria that evaluate **quality and viability of thesis, model and thes[i]s alignment, and
> logic of model assumptions**"

**Three explicit axes:**
1. **Quality and viability of thesis**
2. **MODEL AND THESIS ALIGNMENT** — the model must actually express the thesis. If the variant
   view is "gross margin recovers slower than consensus," the model must have gross margin as a
   visible, driver-level input, not a plug. This is the axis most teams will fail.
3. **Logic of model assumptions** — every assumption justified, not asserted.

Tie-break: *"the Team whose Submission has the overall highest quality and viability."*

## Structure of the rounds
- First round judged by **HFAC and/or Citadel judges**; up to **8 finalist teams** selected.
- **Finalist Round submission** = revised 2-page memo + updated Excel model + a slide deck with
  **no more than 10 minutes of presentation content.**
- Finals: **10-minute presentation + 10-minute Q&A with Citadel judges.** Preliminary round with
  all finalists, then **the 4 highest-scoring teams present AGAIN** in a final round.
- Finalists may not watch another team present before presenting themselves.
- In-person attendance all scheduled days is a condition of participation.

## Other eligibility / compliance notes
- Submissions must be **wholly original work created for this Competition** — cannot be based on
  work performed while working or interning for an employer.
- **Must be based exclusively on PUBLIC information; no MNPI.**
- No confidential information from any entity.
- Citadel gets a broad, irrevocable, perpetual license to use names, school affiliation, and
  Submissions for advertising/publicity/recruiting.
- Submissions shared with HFAC members for First Round evaluation.
- Disputes: binding JAMS arbitration in Florida.
- HFAC members participating in administration/judging are **ineligible to compete.**
