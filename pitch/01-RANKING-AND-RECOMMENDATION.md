## What must be OWNED in the memo (judges reward this; hiding it is fatal)
1. **No CEO.** Narayen announced his transition **March 12, 2026**; as of the **July 17, 2026 8-K**
   no successor was named. **CFO Dan Durn departed June 15, 2026** (interim CFO in seat).
   **This — not AI — is the proximate cause of the de-rating.** It is also the biggest upside
   catalyst when resolved.
2. **You can no longer see Creative Cloud.** In Q1 FY26 Adobe collapsed all segments into ONE
   reportable segment and **stopped disclosing Creative ARR and Document Cloud ARR separately.**
   **This is the structural reason a value trap is possible — deterioration could be masked for
   4–6 quarters.** State it, then explain why RPO/deferred revenue are the workaround.
3. **Organic growth is lower than headline.** Semrush (~$480M ARR, bought April 28 2026 for
   $1.874B = 3.9x ARR) contributed ~0.6pp to Q2's +13%. **Report organic as ~12.4%.**
4. **Absolute net-new Digital Media ARR was FLAT** — FY24 $2.00B vs FY25 ~$1.98B on a 12% larger
   base. The best single number the bears have.
5. **Margin is compressing:** non-GAAP OM 46.2% (FY25) → 45.0% (FY26E) → **44.0% (Q3 guide).**
6. **~7pts of EPS growth is buyback, not operations.**
